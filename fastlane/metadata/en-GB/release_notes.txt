New in 1.4.6:
• Keep each dive’s map and photos together at the top of its details, with an optional title.
• Explore session depth, heart rate and water temperature with linked charts. Zoom to one- or five-minute windows, pinch to zoom, and hide surface intervals.
• Export one session as a Dive Free Backup file. Choose data only or add voice notes, photos and videos.
• Scroll through session details more smoothly.

UI improvements
This follow-up build adds no new features—just cleaner chart spacing, pinch zoom down to five seconds, and a more complete Hide surface intervals option.

Made for the love of diving. All app features are free to use. Core dive recording and logbook features will always be free.
