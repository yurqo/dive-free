This submission only fixes App Store screenshots for iPhone and iPad. Some session-summary screenshots in the approved 1.4.2 listing were captured before the screen finished rendering and appeared blank. They have been replaced with complete, localized screenshots of the existing screen.

There are no app functionality changes, new permissions, new entitlements or in-app purchases in this submission. Promotional text and What's New are unchanged from approved version 1.4.2.

Dive Free is a recreational freediving logbook for Apple Watch, iPhone and iPad. Recording requires a paired Apple Watch. To demo without water on Apple Watch Ultra: start a session, then press Action + Side together to start/stop a manual dive. Stop the session to view its summary and sync it to the paired iPhone.
