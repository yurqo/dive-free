This update improves crowded points on iPhone and iPad session maps. Overlapping start/end boundaries from exactly one dive share a combined marker with that dive’s number. Mixed-dive pairs and groups of three or more points display a distinct point-count badge. Tapping any marker opens individual labels and a complete scrolling list with dive numbers, boundary types and times. Thin lines connect the expanded labels to their original positions. Start/end points that are far apart remain separate, with the existing dashed dive connection. Recorded coordinates, tracks, distances and exports are unchanged.

To review the map changes, open a saved session on iPhone or iPad and tap its header map tile or expand the Location map. Tap a combined dive marker or point-count badge to see the individual points; select a list row to highlight its label, and use the close button to collapse the group. Map points represent surface GPS and estimated dive boundaries, not underwater GPS measurements.

This release adds no permissions, entitlements, purchases or SwiftData/CloudKit storage schema changes. The existing approved screenshots are retained. Screenshot demo data is confined to screenshot/test builds and never changes the user's logbook or Photos library.

Dive Free is a recreational freediving logbook, not a dive computer. All current features are free, and core features will remain free in the future. In-app purchases are not offered.

Recording requires a paired Apple Watch. Depth readings require a compatible Apple Watch Ultra or Series 10/11, with the existing 6 m shallow-depth limit. Other supported watches record GPS and markers.

To demo without water on Apple Watch Ultra: start a session, then press Action + Side together to start/stop a manual dive. Stop the session to review its summary and sync it to the paired iPhone. On iPhone, open that session, use Edit > Edit Details to add a title, and use Photos > Add from Library to attach media. The session header uses those same saved photos and recorded location. A session without photos or GPS adapts to the available content.

Sessions are stored locally and, when enabled, synced through the user's private iCloud account. Photo originals remain in the user's Photos library; references and thumbnails are stored with the logbook. User-requested backup/export operations create local files shared through the system share sheet.

Voice-note editing and on-device transcription are unchanged. Apple Intelligence summaries are optional and only available on supported devices and languages. Provisional notifications are used for a Watch session notice; the aps-environment entitlement supports ActivityKit push-to-start tokens, without marketing pushes.
